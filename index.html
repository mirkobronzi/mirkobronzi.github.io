I’m a Senior Machine Learning Research Scientist at LawZero, where I work on AI safety, with a focus on Scientist AI’s reasoning model. I also study language models’ internal representations and behaviour: what models can report about their own internal states, how they recognize training and evaluation conditions, and what this means for reliable safety assessments.

Previously, I worked at Mila on various applied research projects, and at Nuance on machine learning and natural language processing. I hold a PhD from Roma Tre University, where I studied information extraction and integration from the web.
